# Makan Buddies


# Overview

**Makan Buddies** is an expense tracker and splitter for groups: trips, housemates, couples, dinners, and events. Add expenses as they happen, choose how each one is divided, and the app works out who owes whom, using as few payments as possible to settle up.

# Features

**Groups:** create a group for each trip, household, or event and invite members  
**Flexible splitting:** split equally, by exact amounts, by percentage, or by shares  
**Multiple payers:** record expenses paid by one or several people  
**Simplified debts:** reduces many IOUs to the fewest payments needed to settle  
**(Optional) Multi-currency:** log expenses in different currencies with exchange-rate conversion  
**(Optional) Categories and notes:** tag expenses (food, transport, rent) and attach notes or receipts  
**Balances at a glance:** see who's owed and who owes, per person and per group  
**Settle up:** record payments to clear balances and keep a full history  
**Export:** download expenses as CSV
